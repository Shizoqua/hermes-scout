# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and kind, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the people using and building this project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/Shizoqua/hermes-semantic-search/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting [@Shizoqua](https://github.com/Shizoqua). Reports are handled confidentially.
The maintainer may remove comments, commits or contributions that break this code of conduct, and
may block repeat offenders.
